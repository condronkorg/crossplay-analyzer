How to get your NYT-S token in Chrome

  1. Open nytimes.com and sign in to the NYT account you use for Crossplay.
  2. Right-click the page and select Inspect.
  3. Open Application → Storage → Cookies, select the NYTimes site, and find NYT-S.
  4. Copy its Value, paste it in the box above, and select Connect.

With Google sign-in, leave Remember this NYT cookie encrypted on my account checked to reuse the token on later visits. NYT can expire a token. Forget cookie removes the saved copy. Google sign-in does not connect your NYT account. See our Privacy Policy.
